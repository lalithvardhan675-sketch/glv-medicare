About GLV MediCare

GLV MediCare is a health-information project designed to make reliable medical information easier to explore.

Our goal is to help people learn about health topics and locate hospitals.

Important health notice: This website does not diagnose diseases or prescribe medicines. Symptoms can have different causes. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for diagnosis and treatment. For severe breathing difficulty, chest pain or another medical emergency, seek emergency care immediately.
